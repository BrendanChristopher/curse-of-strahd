# At a Glance
Count Strahd von Zarovich, the Lord of Ravenloft. A Vampire who has ruled over [[Region of Barovia|Barovia]] for hundreds of years.

# Description
Count Strahd von Zarovich is a pale man apparently in his forties. His jet-black hair is kept swept back from his face, resulting in a prominent brow and widow’s peak. His features are gaunt but handsome and his dark red eyes have a deep, intelligent hunger that can make even the strongest man nervous. He tends to dress in exquisite finery, albeit in a somewhat outdated style, though his only adornments are a tasteful ruby-and-gold necklace and a gold signet ring. His physique is lean but muscular, and he is tall—well over six feet tall.

# Lore
Recently awoken from slumber by[[ Doru]], and attacked the [[Village of Barovia]], where he killed [[Kolyan Indirovich]] and bit [[Ireena Kolyana]] during an undead siege that lasted a week.

Met at the [[River Ivlis Crossroads]], where he questioned the party, and TCMS volunteered to go with him as collateral for safe passage from the crossroads for Ireena. 

The [[Town of Vallaki]]may be safe from him, as well may be the Abbey of St Markovia in Krezk.
