An elderly balding man, always wears the vestments of the morninglord. 

# Relationships
Priest of the [[The Church of Barovia]]

# Lore
Locked his vampire spawn son Doru in the basement of the church, asked the party to kill him. They spared him instead. Provided Burial rites for Kolyan.

Donavich suggests the Abbey of St. Markovia in Krezk as an eventual destination. He notes it was once a bastion of good, and may yet still offer some protection.
