# Description
a sad vampire spawn, held in the undercroft of the church of barovia. Resisted his urge to feed on fresh blood.

# Relationships
Son of [[Father Donavich]]

# Lore
After meeting Alanik Ray, and reading Van Richten's guide to Vampires, Doru whipped up a rebellion to kill Strahd. It was unsuccessful, instead waking the vampire lord from a 100 year slumber. He gave the village a warning of 90 days, then attacked with hordes of undead. Sometime during the attack, Strahd turned Doru, and sent him to his father for repentance on his rebellion.

The party retrieved Donavich's holy symbol from Doru, and tested his hunger against fresh blood. The party acted fast, and Doru was able to resist the temptation, holding onto his humanity.
