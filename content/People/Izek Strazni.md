# At a Glance
The Captain of the Black Cloaks of Vallaki, and Baron Vargas' right hand man. Speaking of right hands, his is a devil's, and can throw fire. An enforcer, if there ever was one.

He has been having Blinksy, the local toy maker, make dolls that perfectly resemble [[Ireena Kolyana]].

# Description
A hulking  brute of a man, rough, and adorned in the regal armor of a guard captain. Silver armor, with black trim and a black cloak. His right arm is massive, and above the elbow turns a sickly red color, and into the arm of a devil.
