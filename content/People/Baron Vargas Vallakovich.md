# At a Glance
The burgomaster of the town of vallaki. Baron Vargas Vallakovich, is a ruthless heel who prides himself on his good breeding and finely honed leadership skills. He stages repeated celebrations to foster happiness, and his "All will be well!" catchphrase has become a sad and tiresome punchline. Baron Vallakovich has convinced himself that if he can make everyone in Vallaki happy, the town will slip free of Strahd's dark grasp.

The baron has a brittle ego, and he lashes out at anyone who pokes fun at his festivals or treats him disrespectfully.

Lives in [[Vallakovich Manor]].

# Description
A tall. thin, sleazy looking man. Balding, with a long goatee.
