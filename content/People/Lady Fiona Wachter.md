# At a Glance
A noblewoman in the [[Town of Vallaki]]. Part of an ancient noble house, and has declared herself as an enemy of [[Baron Vargas Vallakovich]].

# Description
An elderly, hawkish women, with shrewd eyes and a fierce gaze. She is intelligent, sophisticated, and carries herself as someone worthy of her station.
