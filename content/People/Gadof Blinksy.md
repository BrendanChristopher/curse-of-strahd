# At a Glance
Runs [[Blinksy's Toys]]

Vallaki's toymaker, Gadof Blinksy, calls himself "a wizard of tiny wonders," but he has been consumed by despair lately because no one seems to like him or want his toys. His fascination for eerie playthings causes most other locals to avoid him. The burgomaster enables Blinsky to stay in business by giving him a couple of gold pieces a month to make festival decorations.

# Description
Blinsky is a heavyset man who wears a moth-eaten jester's cap during store hours, more out of habit than to humor visitors.
