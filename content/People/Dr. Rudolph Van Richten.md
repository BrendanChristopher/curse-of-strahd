# At a Glance
An elderly monster hunter and scholar, who carries himself with poise and grace. What he's doing in Barovia is anyone's guess. 

The name Van Richten is known; the common folk know it; the monsters know it--he is a legend. Imagine if you will, the “scary stories” that parents tell their kids to possibly frighten them away from certain things. Van Richten is the scary story that monsters tell their children.

Goes by the identity of Rictavio in Barovia, so Strahd doesn't discover him. Currently staying at the [[Blue Water Inn]]in the [[Town of Vallaki]].

# Description
An old man in a profession where men die young. Scholarly, gentlemanly, and greying at the temples. Van Richten wears glasses, and has a neat, trimmed appearance, with a hawk-like gaze.

# Lore
Van Richten has authored many books in universe; a series with the working titles "Van Richten's Guide to ____________": Vampires, Ghosts, Werebeasts, Witches, The Ancient Dead, the Created, the Lich, and Demons.

He also has a series titles "Van Richten's Monster Hunter's Compendium" with 3 volumes. 

He is an authority on all things monsters, and slaying them.

This is the Batman or Van Helsing of the DND universe, if Batman straight murdered every opponent he came across, and if Van Helsing was even more successful of a monster hunter.
