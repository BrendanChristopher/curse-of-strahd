# At a Glance
A follower of the Morninglord. To strangers, Father Lucian is a serene, humble, and compassionate priest who is always happy to share a word of praise or a few pearls of wisdom. To those he trusts, Father Lucian is a dedicated and stalwart defender of his community, who nonetheless worries about his ability to keep his congregants safe.

The priest of [[St Andral's Church]]

# Description
An elderly man with gentle eyes. He has white hair and beard, is balding, and has heavy set wrinkles in his face.
