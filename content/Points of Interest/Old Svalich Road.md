# Description
The main road traveling East and West through the [[Region of Barovia]]. Connects all the major settlements, with minor locations and landmarks along the way.

# Major  Locations (East to West)
- [[Village of Barovia]] - A small village in the east of Barovia.
- [[Tser Pool Encampment]] - A small encampment of Vistani. 
- [[Town of Vallaki]] - Major town in the region.
- Village of Krezk - Abbey of St Markovia may be safe for Ireena.


# Minor Locations (East to West)
- [[Gates of Barovia]] - Large Gates flanked by Statues. Opened and shut automatically.
- [[Svalich Woods]] - The wider woods of Barovia. You found a corpse with a compass here.
- [[Barovian Overlook]] - Great view of the valley.
- [[Lugdanas Knoll]] - Ireena's favorite place.
- [[Barovian Scouts]] - 2 scouts, looking for the Lestran family.
- [[Van Richten's Cache]] - Well, found the Lestran family. And a cache of goods.
- [[River Ivlis Crossroads]] - An encounter with Strahd, a sacrificed party member, the march of the dead, the fortunes of ravenloft, and you killed a spy of Strahd.
- [[The Strix]] - A new Party Member, and a giant mechanical bird attack!
- [[The Skeletal Rider]] - The rider you found dead in the woods is wandering Barovia.
- [[Abandoned Watchtower]] - two graves, a tower, and some wolf corpses.
- [[The Ravine]] - Wolves ambushed you, and a large wolf watched from a distance.
- [[Tser Falls]] - A revenant on a bridge.
- Crossroads - East to Castle Ravenloft. West to Vallaki.
- [[Standing Stone, Raven]] - a stone circle, with a 6 foot stone slab. It bears a carving of a raven above a three pointed star.
- [[Durst Windmill]] - You encountered an Old Lady offering Pastries.
- [[The Deep Woods]] - As you trekked further, flocks of ravens joined you overhead.
- [[Ireena's Revelation]] - Ireena sees [[Lake Zarovich]], the same lake she saw in her dream recalled at the [[Tser Pool Encampment]] fire.
- [[The Werewolves' Hunt]] - encounter. Werewolves attacked!
- [[Vallaki Vistani Encampment]] - Dusk Elves and Vistani.
- [[Luna River Bridge]] - You found a small scrap of handkerchief on a log in the river, with embroidered red intials: R.V.R.
- [[Luna River Crossroads]] - An intersection, twig blights, and scarecrows.
- [[Lake Baratok]] - A small lake west of Vallaki. A wagon, some paint, and a tower.
- [[Khazan's Wizard Tower]]- A tower with antimagic, and a rune locked door.
