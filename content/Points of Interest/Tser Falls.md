# Description
On the High Road of the [[Old Svalich Road]]

>You follow the dirt road as it clings to the side of a mountain and ends before an arching bridge of mold-encrusted stone that spans a natural chasm. Gargoyles cloaked in black moss perch on the corners of the bridge, their frowns weatherworn. On the mountainous side of the bridge, a waterfall spills into a misty pool nearly a thousand feet below. The pool feeds a river that meanders into the fog-shrouded pines that blanket the valley.


A nameless revenant stood guard in the middle of the bridge, questioning the players loyalty to Strahd, claiming to be a Knight of the Order of the Silver Dragon.  The revenant shared only that the knights of the Order opposed the lord of Ravenloft in life and advises them to travel to Argynvostholt to the "west, beyond the walled town, then south upon the ash-gray path, where Sir Godfrey Gwilym awaits those who would raise their swords against the darkness that lurks in the castle's depths."
