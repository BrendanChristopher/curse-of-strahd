After returning to [[River Ivlis Crossroads]] after your reading, along the [[Old Svalich Road]], through the mist comes a skeletal warhorse and rider, both clad in clothing remarkably like the commoner you found on the path to the woods. The skeletal rider holds up a rusted lantern that sheds no light. It wandered a short distance down the road, the turned south off the path into the woods.
