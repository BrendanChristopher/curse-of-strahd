At [[Lake Zarovich]], near the Hunter's cabin.

An ancient stone slab, resting on a cracked, mossy stone circle. It bears a carving of a butterfly, the grooves lined with lichen and moss.

Exactly the same setup as [[Standing Stone, Raven]], but with a different carving.
