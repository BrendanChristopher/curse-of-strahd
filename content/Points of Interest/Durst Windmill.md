Along the [[Old Svalich Road]].

>The road soon emerges from the woods once more, veering between two hills. Before long, it bends to the north, hugging the base of a mountain.
>
>The players emerge at the base of the hill upon which **Old Bonegrinder** rests:
>
>You emerge at the base of a mountain, atop which is perched a dilapidated stone windmill, its warped wooden vanes stripped bare.
>
>The onion-domed edifice leans forward and to one side, as though trying to turn away from the stormy gray sky. You see gray brick walls and dirt-covered windows on the upper floors. A decrepit wooden platform encircles the windmill above a flimsy doorway leading to the building's interior.
>
>As the windmill comes into view, you hear the sound of small, wooden wheels rolling across damp cobbles. You trace the lonely sound to a hunched figure bundled in rags, pushing a rickety wooden cart through the fog.

Morgantha is returning from a day trip peddling her wares to the poor hungry dears at the [[Barovian Refugee Camp]].

She lives with her sisters Bella and Offalia in a windmill up the hill.

Morgantha has a few dream pastries left from her workday "filled with the light and love of dreaming" which she offered to the players for free as a first sample. She advises the players that the dream pastries taste best after a warm meal, "especially with a nice bit of wine to wash them down."

Morgantha bid the players a safe journey, and let them know that they can find her outside of Vallaki's gates in the future, should they like to purchase additional pastries.

Before she departed, Morgantha warned the players that the guards at the Vallaki gates are confiscating silver coins and visible silver items from travelers as a tax. "But," she says, tapping her nose with a cheeky, half-toothed grin, "you didn't hear it from me."
