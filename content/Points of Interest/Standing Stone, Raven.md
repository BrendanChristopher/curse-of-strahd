# Description
On the [[Old Svalich Road]], between [[Tser Falls]]and the [[Durst Windmill]] 

>Not far from the road, nestled in the overgrown underbrush, stands a mossy stone slab standing just under six feet in height. It rests on an old, cracked stone circle.  It bears a crude carving of a raven over an engraved three-pointed star, the grooves lined with lichen and moss.

Exactly the same setup as [[Standing Stone, Butterfly]], but a different carving.
