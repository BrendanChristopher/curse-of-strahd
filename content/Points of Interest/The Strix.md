# Description
Halfway down the path from the [[River Ivlis Crossroads]] to [[Tser Pool Encampment]] along the [[Old Svalich Road]], the players hear the sound of a distressed bird cawing from above. A raven with blue-tipped wings—recognizably the same raven that met them in the village of Barovia—then crashes into the road at the players’ feet, visibly wounded. It's wings and torso have been penetrated by several dozen silver barbs, leaving it unable to fly until removed.

The raven’s arrival is followed soon after by a terrible, grating shriek and the arrival of a **greater strix**: a large artificial “bird” crafted of wood, animal skins, burlap, and hundreds of black raven’s feathers. 

The greater strix is joined by two **swarms of lesser strix** . Each lesser strix is an artificial “crow” slightly larger than a raven, crafted of burlap, straw, twigs, and sharp stone teeth.

There was also a lone Barbarian Goliath, Arnolt, fighting the strix.

# The Encounter
The party fought two minor strix and a greater strix. They took them out pretty handily.
