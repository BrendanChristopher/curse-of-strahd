# Description
A four story tower, with a magical door, and golems that operate an elevator. It is falling apart. The door markings matched the platinum ring found where Arabelle disappeared. It has a permanent antimagic spell in it, and a wagon out front. Turns out, the door is trapped, and anyone who tries to open it without the right combination gets hit with a 100 foot long, 5 foot wide bolt of lightening. You learned this when some poor sap tried entering for shelter during the night with two wolves, and got fried. 

Turns out [[Dr. Rudolph Van Richten]]was laying low here, using it as a base of operations. He was using the anti magic field to prevent any magical means of Strahd sensing his presence in the land. After rescuing Arabelle, he brought her here to lay low. They eventually made their way to Vallaki, where she hid out in his wagon, while he brought her supplies every day.

The party spent some hours shoring up the dilapidated flooring, so it is a lot more steady. There are ta large gash in the second floor wall, starting about halfway up, that goes to the third floor. on the second floor it is 8 feet tall by 2 feet wide hole, and on the 3rd floor, about a 1 foot tall by 2 foot hole near the floor. Its large enough to get your head in on the third floor, thats about it. 

While the party was sleeping here, a man in furs approached with two wolves in tow. He tried the door, but the trap activated and fried him. The party used speak with the dead on him, and found out he was looking for shelter for the night, and comes from a place to the northwest of lake Baritok, in a place called Mother Night's Den. The same place Madam Eva mentioned, where a force of great good resides.
