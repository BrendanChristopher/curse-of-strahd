# Description
Along the [[Old Svalich Road]]

As your footsteps squelch into the old, muddy road, you glimpse a flicker of movement in the underwood: a flash of gray fur, amber eyes, and sharp, white teeth. Then, as soon as it appeared, it vanishes, skulking back into the shadows.

As the road curves around the edge of the mountain, the right-hand cliffs fall away, leaving a sheer drop to a wooded ravine 50 feet below. The road, now barely ten feet wide, bends up ahead, doubling back around this ravine before plunging dramatically and winding around a smaller mountain peak. 

Four snarling shadows launch from the underbrush, fangs bared and claws outstretched!

# The Encounter
Four wolves attack from the woodline, attempting to knock the party into the ravine below, where two more wolves await.

One was slain immediately by throk, and 3 were thunderwaved off the cliff by Rusty. The two at the bottom ran off into the woods.
