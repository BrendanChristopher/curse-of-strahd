Located in the [[Town of Vallaki]].

# Description
This uninviting shop is two stories tall and has a sign shaped like a coffin above the front door. All of the window shutters are closed up tight, and a deathly silence surrounds the establishment.

You tracked the bones here. Henrik was forced to steal the bones or be killed by Volenta, a sadistic Vampire.  He paid Milivoj to steal them. You fought Volenta after triggering a trap, and finding the bones, and killed two vampire spawn, heavily wounding Volenta before she escaped. But you escaped with the bones.
