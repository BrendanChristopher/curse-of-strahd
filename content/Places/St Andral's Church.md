# Description
A church of the Morninglord in the [[Town of Vallaki]]. The church's priest is Father Lucian Petrovich, a kind, elderly man. He has taken in an orphan named Yeska, and employs a sullen teenager named Milivoj as the groundskeeper.

The bones that provide hallowed ground to the church had been stolen; you tracked them down through the groundskeeper to the Coffin Maker, Henrik van der Voort, who was pressured into stealing them by a vampire spawn, named Volenta. You fought her and two other vampire spawn in the coffin shop, killing the two spawn, and making Volenta flee. 

# Notable Locations
Chapel - where congregations are held.
Hidden crypt - where the Bones that provide Hallowed Ground are held.
Graveyard - the graveyard for Vallaki.
