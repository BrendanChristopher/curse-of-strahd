A man in furs with wolves approached [[Khazan's Wizard Tower]]while the party was asleep. After a speak with dead spell, from an amulet Imdar recalled having, they learned the man had never been skull-fucked. The party also learned that He was looking for shelter, and he had come from a place called Mother Night's Den, somewhere to the northwest of Lake Baratok. 

This is the same place [[Madam Eva]] spoke of about during [[Fortunes of Ravenloft]] reading.
