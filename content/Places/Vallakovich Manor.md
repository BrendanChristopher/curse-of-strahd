# Description
The Manor of Baron Vargas Vallakovich. All will be well! Located in the Morning District of the [[Town of Vallaki]], this is a house of luxury, especially compared to the dreary and drab houses surrounding it. In the morning district.

The halls are stacked with effigies to be used at the Festival.

# Notable Locations

- Library - The best place in Barovia to research lore.
