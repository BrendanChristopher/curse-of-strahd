# Description
A town in Barovia, which has a church with Hallowed ground. A group of Barovians left the Town of Barovia for Vallaki when the undead started attacking. Gertruda, a young woman, is said to have left here on her own during the night of the siege on the Town of Barovia.

![[vallakistreets.png|750]]

Going into the Morning Gate (or mourning, according to some locals) in the east enters you into the Mo(u)rning district, where the Burgomaster and richest of the townsfolk live. Taking the northern Zarovich Gate (Gate to the Lake for the more fearful) out of town will take you to [[Lake Zarovich]]. The western side of the town, near the Sunset Gate is more rundown, and itis where you will find [[St Andral's Church]]. The [[Blue Water Inn]] is in festival district, where the Town Square is located as well.

Upon arrival you found a shadowed town full of intrigue. The Baron rules the town with fear, while forcing smiles. People are thrown in the stocks for frowning, or speaking ill of the Baron. The refugees are stranded outside the walls, for the Baron believes they are cursed and will bring The Devil's wrath upon them all if he allows them entrance. The church's grounds are no longer hallowed, and vampires have infiltrated the walls of the town. The Captain of the guard, Izek Strazni, has the toy maker Blinksy make dolls that are an uncanny resemblance of Ireena. He has a devil's arm. Rictavio, a traveling ringleader, turned out to be Rudolph Van Richten, who was helping Arabelle, the vistana girl you needed to deliver a present to for her nameday. The baron's son had a teleporter mishap, and his friend is trapped in the ethereal realm, while the Baron thinks the mansion is haunted. Lady Wachter wishes you to join her for some common business.

# Notable Locations
- [[St Andral's Church]] - Hallowed ground, where Ireena would like to go. Sunset District.
- [[Blue Water Inn]] - Local Inn. Friendly owners. In the Festival District.
- [[Coffin Maker's Shop]] - Owned by Henrik van der Voort, where three vampires had convinced him to steal the bones of St Andral. Near the Sunset Gate.
- [[Arasek's Stockyard]] - General Goods Store. Where Van Richten stores his wagon. In the Morning District, near the Gate.
- [[Vallakovich Manor]] - Baron Vargas Vallakovich's home. In the Morning District.
- [[Wachterhouse]] - Lady Fiona Wachter's home. In the Lake District.
- [[Blinksy's Toyshop]] - Blinksy's Shop.
- Refugee Camp - Where the Barovians stay.
- [[Vallaki Vistani Encampment]] - Camp southwest of the town. [[The Dusk Elves]]live here as well.
- [[Lake Zarovich]] - a large, placid lake north of the town.
