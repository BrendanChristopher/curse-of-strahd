# Quest Info

The party discovered the Baron's right hand man, [[Izek Strazni]], has been having the toy maker [[Gadof Blinksy]] craft dolls that resemble [[Ireena Kolyana]]. Izek stared at Ireena in the streets on arrival to Vallaki, while meeting out the Baron's justice on an old woman who had a frown on her face. On Neyavr 4th, the party saw guards putting up wanted posters with Ireena's face on them.. This can't be good...

- [x] Someone is having Blinksy make dolls that resemble Ireena, from all different stages of her life.

# Backstory

* Izek has been having dolls made, resembling Ireena's likeness, changing throughout the years to match her appearance at the time. They are in [[Blinksy's Toys]].
* Izek is the captain of the Vallaki guard, and right hand man to the Baron. The party noticed wanted posters, with a likeness of Ireena, asking for information on her whereabouts, being put up in town.
* The Blue Water Inn was broken into by Izek with a few guards, He was looking for Ireena, and he destroyed a window, a table, some chairs, and scorched the bar. He also rifled thorugh every room. Urwin and Danika did not give up information on Ireena, pretending to never have seen her.
* Izek has kidnapped Ireena from the church in the middle of the day while the party was away. Find out what the hell is going on with this guy!
